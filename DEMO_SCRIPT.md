# StudentReplica: Three Minute Judge Demo

Prepare a synthetic account, a saved roadmap and a previously assessed course.
Keep live AI configured, with the clearly labelled offline fallback available.

## 0:00 To 0:30 Profile And Academics

Sign in and choose Software Engineering, Nursing or another degree. Show the
subject dropdown and Other entry. For a second-year student, show the four-semester
limit, a low core-subject grade, and an ungraded current-semester subject.

## 0:30 To 1:10 AI Direction And Roadmap

Open My Career Plan. Show the assessment, source evidence and recommended goals.
Select an advanced goal and point out its prerequisite warning. Show recovery and
current-coursework tasks before optional work, dated only within free time.

## 1:10 To 1:45 Learning And Evidence

Choose Free only, open a verified resource, and show an AI lesson. Open a prepared
five-question assessment. Explain that four correct answers are required. Show a
failed attempt's revision topics, then a prior pass and its recorded achievement.
University grades remain unchanged; this is formative evidence, not accreditation.

## 1:45 To 2:20 Market Matching

Show a saved live search with employer source links and retrieval time. Compare
strong matches, around-60% matches and interests. Explain proficiency-weighted
coverage, not hiring probability. Select an opening or add its gaps to goals.
If live search is unavailable, show the unverified banner honestly.

## 2:20 To 2:50 CV Studio

Tailor facts for the selected target. Show the assessed learning section, toggle
the optional photo, change included sections, and download PDF and DOCX. AI ranks
existing fact IDs rather than inventing credentials or employment.

## 2:50 To 3:00 Progress And Privacy

Show History and the dated learning milestone. Log out and use the contrasting
demo account: its profile, conversations, quizzes and exports are separate.
