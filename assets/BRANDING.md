# StudentReplica Branding

The application name is **StudentReplica**. The digital twin remains the product's
underlying concept; internal module names and existing student records are unchanged.

## Assets

- `studentreplica-logo.png`: original transparent PNG, 1254 x 1254 pixels.
- The header and sidebar pair this mark with a theme-aware HTML wordmark.
- The browser tab uses the same mark without text.

The paired teal and coral page forms suggest learning, reflection and progress.
The name remains selectable text rather than being embedded in the image.

## Generation

Created with the built-in image generation tool, not the fallback CLI. Final prompt:

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: production app logo symbol for StudentReplica, an academic progress and career planning workspace; used beside a separately rendered wordmark and as a browser favicon.
Primary request: design one polished, distinctive, compact logo mark inspired by two mirrored, offset book-page shapes that together suggest a student's digital replica and upward progress. The unified silhouette should subtly suggest an S or R monogram without literal lettering. Use only two or three bold interlocking geometric shapes, generous clean negative space, crisp edges, and restrained corner rounding. Flat vector-like graphic rendered as a high-quality transparent PNG, not a mockup.
Color palette: saturated teal #087f72 with a smaller warm coral #df796b accent; both colors must remain legible against white and charcoal backgrounds.
Composition/framing: one centered square icon, tightly fitted with only 8 percent transparent margin, balanced weight, visually clear at 32 pixels.
Constraints: genuinely transparent background, no text or wordmark, no frame, no enclosing app tile, no shadows, no gradients, no glow, no 3D, no tiny details, no extra symbols or watermark.
```
